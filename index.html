How to install
  1. Download the file on your phone or tablet, then open it.

  3. Choose Install.

Latest update

Version 0.9.0

October 18, 2026

Improved

  • Storybones is easier to use with a keyboard, a screen reader, or a finger: everything you tap is at least 44 by 44 pixels, and screen readers say the same words you see.
  • Boxes you type in or pick from have a clearer edge, and the Tips bulb shows a ring when its tips are on.
  • High contrast is stronger for people with color blindness, and so are the right and wrong colors in courses.
  • The accent colors in Slate & Clay and Driftwood & Teal are a shade deeper, so they stand out for people with color blindness too.
  • With Larger text on a phone, nothing runs off the side of the screen: tabs and labels move to a new line instead.
  • Character and book cards open from anywhere on the card.
  • Exporting a whole project with lots of pictures is faster.

Fixed

  • On a phone, a character’s name had almost no room beside the buttons next to it.
  • The “primary” label on an outline was hard to read when that outline was open.

Every earlier update is in the app, under What’s New.
