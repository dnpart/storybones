About Storybones

Build the bones, then write the story.

Our mission

Storybones helps writers get started and learn how stories are built. Outline your plot, shape your characters, and map your world, with guidance at every step. It’s free, it works offline, and your work stays on your device.

How Storybones was made

Storybones was ideated by humans. Every idea, feature, and design in it came from people: writers, building the tool we wished we had. The app’s code was written with help from Claude Code, an AI coding tool made by Anthropic.

A person reviewed, tested, and approved every part of Storybones before launch. The same goes for every update.

Your writing stays yours

Storybones has no AI features. Nothing you write is sent to an AI, and nothing you write is used to train one. Your projects stay on your device unless you choose to back them up or sync them to your own cloud storage.

Our view on AI

We’re not anti-AI, but we are pro-regulation. AI can be an amazing tool: it helped us build the app we dreamed of. It also needs real rules that stop corporations from harming our planet and keep AI from being used for violence.

We use AI thoughtfully, and we try to be as kind to the planet and as ethical as we can. One small example: Storybones runs on your device, so there are no servers running for it around the clock.

Keep Storybones free

No ads, no subscriptions, and we never sell your data. If Storybones helps you, a small tip helps keep it free for everyone.
