Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Storybones is built so your work stays yours. Here’s exactly what that means, for the Storybones app and for this website.

What stays on your device

Everything you make: projects, books, outlines, characters, pictures, your profile, and your settings. Storybones has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. We can’t see your work, and neither can anyone else.

The one thing that can leave it

A message you choose to send with Send feedback. It goes to Web3Forms, a form service that emails it to us. It carries what you typed, the type you picked, your email if you added one, and the app version and device type if you ticked that box. Nothing else is sent, and never your projects. Web3Forms handles it under its own privacy policy.

Backups

A backup is a file you save yourself, wherever you choose. Storybones doesn’t keep a copy anywhere else.

On Android, your phone may also back up Storybones with your other apps, to your own Google account, if Android’s backup is turned on. That copy brings your work back if you reset or replace your phone. Only you can reach it: we never see it, and with a screen lock set, Android encrypts it so only you can open it. You can turn Android’s backup on or off in your phone’s settings.

AI

Storybones has no AI features. Nothing you write is sent to an AI, and nothing you write is used to train one.

This website

This website has no cookies, no analytics, and no tracking, and it loads nothing from other websites. It’s hosted by GitHub, which, like every web host, records basic details of each visit, such as your IP address, to keep its service running and secure. GitHub handles those under its own privacy statement. Downloads come from GitHub too.

If anything changes

If Storybones ever adds something that goes online, such as syncing with your own cloud storage, this page and the app’s Privacy page will say so first, and you’ll choose whether to turn it on.

Questions? Use Send feedback in the app, or email storybones.waffle068@passinbox.com.
