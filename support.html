Support

Help with installing, backups, and anything else.

Installing

Android: download the file on your phone or tablet, then open it. If your device says it can’t install apps from your browser, choose Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back, and choose Install.

Using Brave on Android? Brave can turn the download into a request to sign in to GitHub. You don’t need a GitHub account: open this website in Chrome or another browser, and download from there.

Windows: open the file you downloaded. If you see Windows protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway, and follow the steps.

Your device asks first because Storybones isn’t in the app stores yet.

Updating

Download the newest version from the home page and install it the same way. It replaces the old version, and your work stays. To see which version you have, open Settings › About in the app.

Backing up

Your work is saved on your device. To keep a copy safe, open Settings › Backups and choose Back up now, then save the file somewhere other than the device, such as cloud storage, email, or a USB drive. Home reminds you when a backup is due.

Moving to a new device

  1. On the old device, back up (see above).
  2. Put the backup file somewhere the new device can reach it.
  3. Install Storybones on the new device.
  4. Open Settings › Backups and choose Restore from a backup file, then pick the file.

Learning Storybones

On Home, Take the tour shows you around in two minutes. Learn has a short lesson on every part of Storybones, and the ? button on a screen opens its lesson.

Something wrong?

In the app, open Settings › Send feedback and tell us what happened. Tick the box to include your app version and device type: it helps us find the problem. If the app won’t open, email storybones.waffle068@passinbox.com instead, and say which device you use.

Uninstalling

Back up first. On Windows, open Settings › Apps › Installed apps, find Storybones, and choose Uninstall. It asks whether to delete your Storybones data too: leave that unticked to keep your work for a later reinstall. On Android, press and hold the Storybones icon, choose App info, then Uninstall. Uninstalling on Android removes everything saved in Storybones.
